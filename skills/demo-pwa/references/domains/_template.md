# Domain playbook: <trade>

Copy to add a trade. Keep it concrete: what to build when someone says "demo para <trade>".

## Matches
Businesses and phrases this covers.

## Roles and areas
| Role | Area | Can do |
|---|---|---|

## Entities
Fields, statuses as a closed list with label and tone, **allowed transitions**, rules.

## Screens
Customer (phone first, max 5 tabs) and admin (desktop), with the main question each screen answers.

## Flows to show in the meeting
3 to 5 flows, each with the failure path.

## Seed data
What realistic data looks like in Mexico for this trade: names, products, units, prices, schedules, dates. Include records in every status.

## Concept seeds for the design
Objects, documents or tools of the trade the visual concept can come from. Give impeccable these as raw material, not as a decision.

## Pitfalls
What goes wrong in this business and what owners care about.

## Not built by default
Things to list as next steps.
